## The Manual Collection Sweep

The manual collection sweep is the act of walking over the dropped cash to pick it up. The sweep takes ten to thirty seconds depending on the floor size, and the compounding gain from sweeping is significant. Every minute you leave unbanked cash on the floor is a minute the AFK layer is not crediting your banked balance.

The manual collection sweep is the foundation of the offline earnings. The player who sweeps the floor on every login and logout credits the AFK layer with the banked balance, which compounds the overnight accumulation. The player who ignores the sweep loses the same compounding.

The manual collection sweep is the same on PC and mobile. The walk-over to collect cash works the same way with WASD on PC as with the on-screen joystick on mobile. The time cost is similar, and the compounding gain is the same.

## The Common Cash Flow Mistakes

The most common cash flow mistake is leaving cash on the floor. The AFK timer credits the banked balance, not the floor cash. The cure is to sweep the floor on every login and logout. The pattern is consistent with what the wiki tracks in the live game snapshot and what the developer confirms through the official Roblox game page.

The second most common mistake is leaving the game in a bottlenecked configuration. The AFK layer multiplies the bottleneck along with the rest of the configuration, so a roller that is too slow for the new butter will drag the overnight accumulation down. The cure is to verify the roller can cover the floor before logging out.

The third most common mistake is force-closing the Roblox app on mobile. Force-closing terminates the connection to the server, which can in some edge cases interrupt the offline accumulation timer. Letting the app run in the background and locking the screen is the correct way to save battery while keeping the offline accumulation running.
