## The First Five Minutes

The first five minutes of every Crunch My Butter session are identical. You join a server, the game drops you next to your starter Salted Butter, and you press the roller into it. The shell cracks audibly, cash drops on the floor, and you walk over the cash to collect it.

The first five minutes should not include browsing the upgrade menu. The upgrade menu is informative, but at minute five the prices are not yet affordable and the time you spend browsing is time you are not collecting cash. Get the loop running first, then optimize.

## The Collection Sweep

The collection sweep is the act of walking over the dropped cash to pick it up. The sweep takes ten to thirty seconds depending on the floor size, and the compounding gain from sweeping is significant. Every minute you leave unbanked cash on the floor is a minute the AFK layer is not crediting your banked balance.

The collection sweep is the foundation of the offline earnings. The player who sweeps the floor on every login and logout credits the AFK layer with the banked balance, which compounds the overnight accumulation. The player who ignores the sweep loses the same compounding.

The collection sweep is the same on PC and mobile. The walk-over to collect cash works the same way with WASD on PC as with the on-screen joystick on mobile. The time cost is similar, and the compounding gain is the same.

## The Reinvestment Cadence

The reinvestment cadence is the rhythm of buying the next unlock as soon as the banked balance is high enough. The cadence should be after every crunch session, which means the player checks the upgrade menu, buys the next unlock if affordable, and returns to crunching.

The reinvestment cadence is the foundation of the compounding loop. The player who follows the cadence reinvests every cash balance into the next unlock, which compounds the AFK layer and the offline earnings. The player who deviates from the cadence by hoarding cash loses compounding minutes every idle minute.

The reinvestment cadence is the same for every tier: buy the next affordable upgrade as soon as the prompt allows. The exception is when the roller is bottlenecking the butter, in which case the player should save for the roller upgrade instead.

## The Bottleneck Recognition

The bottleneck recognition is the test that determines whether the butter or the roller is the bottleneck. The test is simple: if the floor is full of uncrunched butter, the roller is the bottleneck. If the roller is sweeping the floor between respawns and there are no uncrunched butters, the butter is the bottleneck.

The bottleneck recognition is the foundation of the decision framework. The player who recognizes the bottleneck and invests in the track that resolves it will out-compound the player who invests in arbitrary tracks. The recognition is the player's defense against over-investment in the wrong track.

The bottleneck recognition is qualitative because the exact roller coverage is not published. The principle is that the roller should be able to keep up with the butter respawn rate, and the floor should be empty of uncrunched butter when the roller is sweeping.

## The CPM Calculation

The CPM calculation is the foundation of the optimization framework. The player who measures CPM consistently can identify when the next upgrade will increase CPM by the largest margin. The player who does not measure CPM is flying blind and may invest in arbitrary tracks.
