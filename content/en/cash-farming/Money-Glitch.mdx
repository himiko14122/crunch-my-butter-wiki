If cash appears to disappear, the most common cause is that the cash is on the floor, not in the bank. The AFK layer credits the banked balance, not the floor cash, which means the player who does not sweep the floor sees the banked balance stay flat while cash drops accumulate on the floor. The cure is to sweep the floor on every login and logout.

The second most common cause is that the player has a connection issue. A connection issue can cause the client to lose sync with the server, which means the client may show a balance that differs from the server's balance. The cure is to log out and log back in, which forces the client to resync with the server.

The third most common cause is that the developer has rolled back a server issue. Server issues are rare but possible, and the developer may roll back the affected servers to a previous state. The cure is to wait for the developer to fix the issue and to check the official Roblox game page for any announcements.

The fourth most common cause is that the player has logged into a different Roblox account. Crunch My Butter's progression is tied to the Roblox account, not the device. The player who logged into a different account sees a different balance. The cure is to log into the correct account.
