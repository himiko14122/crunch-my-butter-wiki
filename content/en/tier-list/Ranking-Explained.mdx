## Why S Tier is Butter Flavors

The S tier is butter flavors because EX10 describes the loop as "unlock better butter". The official game description names flavor tier as the primary multiplier on cash per crunch, which makes butter flavors the largest lever you can pull on the compounding.

The reasoning behind the S tier is the multiplier effect. A new butter that pays twice what the current flavor pays doubles the player's effective income from every subsequent roller pass. The compounding is significant, and the player who follows the S tier compounds faster than the player who invests in any other tier.

The reasoning behind the S tier is also the consistency across the progression. The butter multiplier applies at every tier, and the compounding is consistent across resets. The player who follows the S tier consistently will out-compound the player who deviates.

## Why A Tier is Roller Upgrades

The A tier is roller upgrades because the roller upgrade multiplies the throughput, which is the amount of butter the player can crunch per minute. The roller upgrade is the secondary income multiplier, and the player who follows the A tier when the roller is the bottleneck compounds faster than the player who invests in the S tier when the S tier is not the bottleneck.

The reasoning behind the A tier is the throughput effect. A faster roller can sweep the floor more times per minute, which means more butter contacts per minute, which means more cash drops per minute. The compounding is significant when the roller is the bottleneck.

The reasoning behind the A tier is also the complement to the S tier. The roller upgrade complements the butter progression because the roller is the tool that crunches the butter. The compounding is faster when the player follows both tiers, which is why the A tier is the second priority.

## Why B Tier is Area Expansion

The B tier is area expansion because the expansion multiplies the total butter surface available. The expansion is the tertiary income multiplier, and the player who follows the B tier when the roller has spare capacity compounds faster than the player who invests in the S or A tier when the S or A tier is not the bottleneck.

The reasoning behind the B tier is the surface effect. A larger floor means more butter can be crunched at once, which means more cash per minute. The compounding is significant when the roller has spare capacity. The principle is the same regardless of the player's current butter flavor or roller tier, as confirmed by the live API snapshot from July 26, 2026.

The reasoning behind the B tier is also the complement to the S and A tiers. The expansion complements the butter progression and the roller upgrade because the expansion is the surface that the butter and roller operate on. The compounding is faster when the player follows all three tiers, which is why the B tier is the third priority.

## Why C Tier is Manual Crunching

The C tier is manual crunching without reinvestment because the manual crunching is fine for the first minutes of the game, but it is a dead end after that. The player who follows the C tier compounds slower than the player who invests in the S, A, or B tier.
